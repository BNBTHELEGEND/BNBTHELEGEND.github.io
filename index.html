THE BASICS

How BNB Rummy works

01

Form two teams

Four players make two teams of two, with teammates sitting opposite each other.

02

Build combinations

Create sequences and groups using two standard decks plus two jokers.

03

Open at 71+

Your opening must reach at least 71 points and contain a clean sequence of three or more cards.

04

Control the table

After opening, players can extend combinations across the shared table, making every card matter.

KEY RULES

The rules at a glance

Jokers

Jokers are completely wild, with a maximum of one joker in a combination. An unplayed joker left in hand is worth 50 points.

Discarding

A card that can extend a table combination generally cannot be discarded unless the player holds both physical copies.

Opening escalation

If an unopened player opens with a score, the next unopened player must open higher.

Finishing

When a player legally gets rid of their cards, the round ends immediately and the opposing team's remaining cards are scored.
